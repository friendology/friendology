Learning Paths

A gentle plan that keeps every part of thinking active — counting and language, logic and memory, space and imagination. No accounts, no pressure, no fees. Just a suggested order, so you always know what to play next.

Often people talk about being “left-brained” or “right-brained.” In truth, almost every real task uses both sides together — reading a map, solving a puzzle, telling a story. So friendology doesn’t try to label anyone as one type. The aim is simpler and kinder: a rounded mix of skills, practised a little at a time.
