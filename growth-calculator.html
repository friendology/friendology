How adult healthy weight is estimated

Adult healthy weight uses Body Mass Index: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered healthy.

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9Overweight
30.0 and aboveObesity
This is an educational estimate, not medical advice.
Genetics, puberty timing, and individual growth curves matter more than any single number. A child steadily following their own percentile is usually healthy. For real assessment, consult a pediatrician or doctor — they use full WHO/CDC percentile charts.
