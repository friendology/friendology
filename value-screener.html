Value Screener

A fundamental + technical screen for NSE stocks. It scores each name on how cheap, healthy and stabilising it looks, applies a strict value gate, and ranks the results — so you find value with confirmation, not a falling knife.

Educational screener — not investment advice. It only scores the numbers you enter; it doesn't recommend buying or selling. Verify everything on a primary source (screener.in, Tickertape, NSE), and check promoter holding & pledging separately — they aren't in here. Your data stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Add your stocks. Type them into the table below, or paste / import a CSV. Get the figures free from screener.in, Tickertape or nseindia.com. Tap “Load sample data” to see the exact format, then replace it with your own.
  2. Fill in what you have. Fundamentals drive the score; the value gate needs at least P/E, P/B, D/E and ROE. Technicals (RSI, 50/200-day averages, 52-week high/low) are optional — leave them blank and that row is scored on fundamentals alone. Any blank cell is skipped, never guessed.
  3. Set the knobs and run. Pick the fundamentals-vs-technicals split and any minimum scores, then tap Run screen. Hover a column header for what it means, and export the ranked table as CSV or Excel.

It only scores the numbers you enter — it never fetches live prices and never tells you to buy or sell. Treat the output as a shortlist to research, not a recommendation.

How the score works
  • Fundamental score (0–100) = valuation 34% · quality 26% · health 20% · growth 14% · income 6%. Cheaper P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, PEG score higher; higher ROE, margins, lower debt, steady growth and dividends add quality.
  • Technical score (0–100) = trend 40% · momentum 30% · entry 20% · volume 10%. Rewards price above its 200-DMA, an RSI sweet-spot (~45–65), a lower-middle position in the 52-week range, and volume confirmation.
  • Combined = your fundamentals/technicals split (default 65/35). Rows with no technicals are scored on fundamentals alone.
  • Value gate (must pass all): P/E 0–45, P/B ≤ 8, D/E ≤ 2.5, ROE ≥ 8%. Fails the gate → labelled “Not value”, however good the technicals.
  • Verdict: ≥75 Strong Value Buy · ≥65 Value Buy · ≥55 Value Watch · else Neutral (gate-passers only).
